Relational Database Schema & System Architecture

1. System Flow Architecture

[Student Interaction] ──▶ [Student Diary / Quiz Attempts / Search Queries] │ ▼ [Analytics Engine] (Evaluates Accuracy, Time Spent, Topic Failures) │ ▼ [LLM Prompt Orchestrator (Gemini)] (Injects Student Gaps, Pedagogical Instruction, Bloom Level) │ ▼ ┌────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────┐ ▼ ▼ [Adaptive Question Generator] [Personalized Study Plan & Explanations] (Generates MCQs for Weak Topics) (Feeds back to Student Diary & Teacher Alerts)

2. Normalized Database Entities & DDL Specification

users

PK: user_id (UUID) | email, password_hash, role [ADMIN, TEACHER, STUDENT], full_name, avatar_url, created_at

student_diary

PK: diary_id | FK: student_id, subject_id | entry_date, topic_covered, study_duration_min, self_notes, teacher_feedback, ai_sentiment

teacher_diary

PK: teach_log_id | FK: teacher_id, course_id, subject_id | log_date, class_name, lesson_topic, attendance_count, homework_assigned

question_bank

PK: question_id | FK: subject_id, created_by | topic, question_text, question_type [MCQ, TF], difficulty [EASY, MED, HARD], options_json, correct_answer, explanation, marks

quizzes & quiz_attempts

PK: attempt_id | FK: quiz_id, student_id | score, total_marks, accuracy_pct, time_spent_sec, topic_breakdown_json, ai_diagnostic_notes

ai_recommendations

PK: rec_id | FK: student_id, topic_id | trigger_reason, recommended_action, resource_link, mastery_before, mastery_after, status
